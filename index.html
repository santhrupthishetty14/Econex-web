About Econex

Driving Environmental Engineering Excellence

Econex Env Solutions LLP is an environmental engineering firm specializing in waste remediation and sustainable environmental setups. We design, supply, and operate systems in partnership with leading manufacturers, transitioning organizations toward zero waste.

Our mission is to engineer high-integrity infrastructure that prevents waste accumulation in landfills. Through specialized consultation and advanced machinery partners, we empower enterprises, commercial complexes, and government agencies to process organic, liquid, and plastic waste close to the source, recycling materials back into the production cycle.

We are driven by deep professional accountability. Every setup we deliver complies with strict regional pollution boards. We emphasize maximum material recovery, clean working environments, durability under heavy duty cycles, and clear environmental audit logging.

Customizable OWC Setups

In partnership with specialized technology manufacturers, we integrate, install, and commission Organic Waste Converter setups tailored to your specific daily volume requirements and spatial constraints.

Tailored System Design: Layouts customized based on daily organic waste volumes and site-specific civil/electrical configurations.
Heavy-Duty Durability: Mixing chambers and blades constructed using corrosion-resistant industrial stainless steel.
PLC Automation: Simple control interfaces with built-in safety interlocks, overload protection, and automated cycling.
Odor Mitigation: Double-filtration exhaust configurations and negative-pressure ventilation to maintain fresh working air.
